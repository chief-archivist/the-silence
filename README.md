# THE SILENCE

**A meteor is coming.**  

You know it. Your scientists *will* know it.  
The question is whether your civilization can hold together long enough to escape.

Guide your species from a fragile Earth-bound society to a Dyson Swarm civilization. Unlock technologies. Allocate your population across ideological, biological, and technological structures. Balance energy, growth, and societal stability as the clock ticks toward Year 2500.

But surviving the meteor is just the beginning. Once you reach the stars, you start broadcasting. **Something out there is listening.**

---

### What this game is:

- An incremental resource management game where **how you build your society matters as much as how fast**.
- The UI lies to you. The ticker watches you. The logs remember civilizations you never played. Most of them end the same way.

**Phase 1 is playable now.** The Hunters are coming.
